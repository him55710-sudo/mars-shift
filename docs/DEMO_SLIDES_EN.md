# Seven-slide project demonstration

The implemented deck is `/briefing`. There are exactly seven slides including the cover. All figures on slide 4 are calculated by the same engine as the live app.

1. **MARS SHIFT — When Mars changes, your map changes.** Real Jezero orbital imagery. Adaptive EVA planning with traceable evidence and a return margin.
2. **One next action. A visible way home.** The actual crew view shows the next checkpoint, direct return time and projected reserve. Prepare → Traverse → Science → Return. Mission Control selects the mission; Crew view guides the field rehearsal.
3. **Three data types. One small, real place.** HiRISE optical (~0.3 m source), topography (1 m → 5 m planning cells) and THEMIS (100 m context). No invented fine thermal detail.
4. **Compare the whole round trip.** Actual baseline table for shortest, lower exposure and mission-aware paths. State that time and reserve are simulated under explicit assumptions.
5. **Hold. Verify. Compare. Adopt.** In the crew view, verify position and buddy, compare a remaining-mission route and explicitly accept it. In the planning benchmark, Insert the 35 m simulated exclusion. Prior mission path intersects 12 cells; new mission route adds 99.1 m and avoids the event. A path may also fail constraints.
6. **Keep uncertainty in the picture.** Observed / inferred / simulated / unknown. No safety probabilities, real-time Mars weather or imminent-collapse forecast.
7. **Preserve the science. Preserve a way home.** Existing NASA tools are reused. Cite data, literature, limitations and AI assistance. Future: registered repeat observations and validated human-resource models.
